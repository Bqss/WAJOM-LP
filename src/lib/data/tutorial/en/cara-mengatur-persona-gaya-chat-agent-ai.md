The **Persona** tab in Agent AI is used to define your assistant's unique identity, personality (_brand voice_), and communication ethical boundaries. With the right persona configuration, Agent AI will greet customers just like a friendly, professional company representative who understands their boundaries and authority.

## Prerequisites

✅ Your business identity profile and brand communication style (_brand tone_) are already defined.

✅ Your account has access to a WhatsApp instance in the Wajom portal.

---

## 1. Setting Business Identity

1.  Open the **Agent AI** menu in the Wajom portal, then click the **Persona** tab.
2.  In the **01 Identity** section, fill in the following parameters:
3.  - **Business Name**: Enter the brand or store name that will be introduced to customers (example: `Senja Coffee Shop` or `Wajom`).
4.  - **Company Name**: Enter your company's official legal entity name (example: `PT Senja Makmur Abadi`).

![borang tetapan Identitas Bisnis](/images/agent-ai/persona-01-identitas.png)

---

## 2. Setting Up AI Behavior Instructions (System Prompt)

In the **02 AI Behavior** section, write comprehensive instructions as if you were conducting an orientation for a new _customer service_ staff member:

![Area Input Instruksi Perilaku AI dan Bahasa Balasan](/images/agent-ai/persona-02-perilaku.png)

### Key Elements in AI Behavior:

1. **Role & Character**: _Example:_ _"You are a friendly and welcoming virtual assistant named Senja from Senja Coffee Shop. Your job is to answer questions about the coffee menu, opening hours, and drink recommendations."_ 2. **Tone of Voice**: _Example:_ _"Use casual, warm, and polite language. Use relevant emojis moderately (☕, 😊, 🙏) to create a friendly atmosphere."_ 3. **Boundaries & Restrictions (Do's & Don'ts)**: _Example:_ _"Never promise discounts outside official promotions. If a customer reports damaged goods or a delivery complaint, immediately advise them to connect with a human agent."_

### Setting Response Language

Select the primary language preference used by the bot: - **Indonesian**: The agent always replies in standard or casual Indonesian according to the behavioral instructions. - **Automatic (follow customer's language)**: The agent detects the language used by the customer (Indonesian, English, Malay, etc.) and replies in the same language. - **English**: The agent replies in international English. - **Malay**: The agent replies in Malay.

---

## 3. Enabling Human-like Tone and Welcome Message

Scroll to the bottom of the Persona tab:

![tetapan Gaya Bicara Seperti Manusia dan mesej Sambutan](/images/agent-ai/persona-03-sambutan.png)

### Human-like Tone

- Turn on the **Human-like tone** toggle switch. - When enabled, the AI model avoids rigid, robotic clichés and adopts sentence structures that are more flexible, natural, and warm, mirroring real human interactions.

### Welcome Message

- In the **03 Welcome message** section, enter the opening text automatically sent to customers when they first message your WhatsApp number. - Leave this field blank if you prefer the AI to respond directly and contextually to the customer's initial message without a separate greeting message.

---

## 4. Checking the Preview Panel and Saving Changes

1.  Check the **Preview** panel on the right side of the screen to review a summary of the identity and character you have just configured.

![Persona Preview Panel](/images/agent-ai/persona-04-pratinjau.png)

1.  Once all persona settings are correct, click the blue **Save** button on the bottom bar to apply the configuration.
