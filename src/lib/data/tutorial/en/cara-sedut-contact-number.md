## Introduction to Wajom Group Grabber

Wajom Group Grabber is a feature that allows you to extract phone numbers from WhatsApp groups easily and safely. This feature is very useful for:

-   **Building a Database**: Collect phone numbers for marketing campaigns
-   **Team Management**: Extract team members' numbers from business groups
-   **Lead Generation**: Acquire new prospects from relevant groups

## ⚠️ Important Warning

> **Alert**: Alert! The "Wajom Group Grabber" feature was created so leaders can easily extract their team members' phone numbers from their own business WhatsApp groups

Stealing prospects from other business WhatsApp groups & other leaders is strictly prohibited!

> **Alert**: Alert! This feature cannot be used on the Wajom FREE Trial. You must subscribe to a paid Wajom plan to extract millions of database contacts from WhatsApp groups

## Prerequisites

-   A paid Wajom account (not available for the FREE Trial)
-   A WhatsApp number that has already joined the groups you want to extract from
-   An active WhatsApp Web session
-   Wajom Contact Grabber Plugin (refer to Tutorial 8)

### Step 5: Managing the CSV File

7. Open the downloaded CSV file on your laptop

No 1 - The correct format for writing names. Make sure there is a "name" column in the CSV file

No 2 - Ensure the spelling is correctly set to "phone"

No 3 - Incorrect WhatsApp number format. The letter "E" should not appear

To fix the format, press Ctrl + 1

## Tips and Best Practices

### ✅ Do’s

-   Use a personal number that has joined many delivery groups and groups with real people
-   Extract from your own business groups or groups where you have permission
-   Rename the CSV file with the group name and date for easy tracking
-   Ensure the phone number formatting is correct before saving

### ❌ Don’ts

-   Do not extract from other business leaders' groups without permission
-   Do not use for spam or unethical activities
-   Do not extract from groups containing fake numbers or international scam numbers
