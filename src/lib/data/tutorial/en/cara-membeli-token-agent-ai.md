Agent AI uses token quota to read customer messages, analyze the knowledge base, and generate automated replies on WhatsApp. Each time Agent AI replies, your account's token balance will be deducted according to the text length and selected AI reasoning depth. When the token balance runs out, Agent AI will automatically stop replying.

This guide explains how to check your token balance in the Agent AI dashboard, choose the right token package, and complete the token purchase process.

## Prerequisites

✅ Your Wajom account is logged in to the official portal at `portal.wajom.co`.

✅ Your business WhatsApp number is connected.

✅ A payment method (QRIS, Virtual Account, or E-Wallet) is ready to use.

---

## 1. Checking Agent AI Remaining Token Balance

1.  Open the Wajom portal at [portal.wajom.co](https://portal.wajom.co).
2.  Select your WhatsApp instance, then go to the **Agent AI** menu > **Summary** tab.
3.  Next to the status toggle switch card, locate the **AI Token Balance** card.
4.  Inside the card, you will find your total active token quota along with a **Top Up Balance** link.

![AI Token Balance Card on Summary Tab](/images/agent-ai/beli-token-01-saldo.png)

> **Tip**: We recommend topping up your balance when the remaining tokens fall below 100,000 tokens so that Agent AI does not suddenly stop serving customers. You can also enable automated low-balance alerts via the [Settings](/agent-ai/cara-mengaktifkan#9-menetapkan-mesej-cadangan-dan-notifikasi-admin) menu.

---

## 2. Opening the Token Purchase Page

1.  Click the blue text link **Top Up Balance** located on the AI Token Balance card in the **Summary** tab.
2.  You will be redirected to the official token checkout page at `/checkout-token`.
3.  On this page, the system displays your current token balance information and the official Wajom token package catalog.

---

## 3. Choosing the Right Token Package

In the **Select Token Package** section, choose the most efficient package for your business's incoming message volume:

![List of Token Packages on Checkout Page](/images/agent-ai/beli-token-02-paket.png)

| Token Package | Token Amount | Promo Price | Estimated Usage | Business Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **STARTER 1M** | 1,000,000 tokens | Rp39,000 | ± 1,500 – 2,500 replies | SMEs or businesses just starting to test the bot. |
| **ADVANCE 2M** | 2,000,000 tokens | Rp66,000 | ± 3,000 – 5,000 replies | Growing SMEs with regular chats. |
| **GROWTH 3M** | 3,000,000 tokens | Rp99,000 | ± 5,000 – 8,000 replies | Mid-sized businesses with increasing customer support traffic. |
| **PRO 10M** | 10,000,000 tokens | Rp299,000 | ± 15,000 – 25,000 replies | **Best Seller:** Businesses with high interaction volume. |
| **ENTERPRISE 40M** | 40,000,000 tokens | Rp999,000 | ± 60,000 – 100,000 replies | Large scale operations, major retail stores, and promotional campaigns. |

1. Select the token package you want. 2. Click the **Select** button on that package card. The selected card will automatically be highlighted in blue.

---

## 4. Completing Payment

1.  Look at the right-hand panel in the **Order Summary** section.
2.  Ensure the selected package name, token amount, and billing amount are correct.
3.  Select an instant payment method:
4.  - **QRIS**: Instant payment via GoPay, OVO, Dana, ShopeePay, or BCA Mobile.
5.  - **Virtual Account (VA)**: Automatic bank transfer via BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI, or Permata.
6.  - **E-Wallet**: According to active payment channels.
7.  Click the **Pay Now** button to display the QRIS code or Virtual Account number.
8.  Complete the payment before the invoice expiration time.

![Token Purchase Order Summary Panel](/images/agent-ai/beli-token-03-checkout.png)

---

## 5. Verifying Token Balance Addition

After the payment is successfully completed: 1. Return to the [Agent AI Summary](/agent-ai/index) page. 2. Check the number on the **AI Token Balance** card. The balance amount will automatically increase in _real-time_ without needing a manual refresh. 3. Your Agent AI is immediately ready to continue responding to customer conversations seamlessly.
