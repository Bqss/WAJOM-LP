## Introduction to CSV Import

The CSV Import feature allows you to import thousands of phone numbers into a Wajom List in just a few seconds. This is very useful for:

-   **Database Management**: Organize contacts into different lists
-   **Marketing Campaigns**: Prepare target audiences for campaigns
-   **Automation**: Streamline contact management processes

## Prospect Database Sources

You can obtain phone numbers from various sources:

-   ✅ WhatsApp Groups (using Wajom Group Grabber)
-   ✅ Facebook Groups and Pages
-   ✅ Website registration forms
-   ✅ Google My Business inquiries
-   ✅ LinkedIn connections
-   ✅ Event registrations
-   ✅ Existing customer databases

## Prerequisites

-   An active Wajom account
-   A CSV file with the correct format (columns: name, phone)
-   A WhatsApp number already connected to Wajom

## Tips and Best Practices

### ✅ Do’s

-   Ensure the CSV format is correct with "name" and "phone" columns
-   Use descriptive names for your lists (e.g., “FB Group Prospects”, “Event Database”)
-   Verify phone numbers are properly formatted before uploading
-   Back up your CSV file before uploading for safety

### ❌ Don’ts

-   Do not upload overly large CSV files all at once (maximum 1,000 contacts per batch)
-   Do not use special characters in contact names
-   Do not upload duplicate contacts into the same list

## Correct CSV Format

Ensure your CSV file contains:

```
name,phone
Ahmad Ali,60123456789
Siti Fatimah,60198765432
```
