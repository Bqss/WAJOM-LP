## Introduction to WhatsApp Warm Up

Warm up new WhatsApp numbers to prevent them from getting banned. Warm up old numbers that haven't been used for a long time.

The warm-up process is an essential step to ensure your WhatsApp number is not flagged as spam by WhatsApp's system.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the warm-up process, make sure:

-   You have access to the Wajom portal
-   The WhatsApp number is already registered in the system
-   You understand the importance of the warm-up process to avoid bans

## Tips and Practical Guide

**Full Automation**: Wajom's warm-up automation helps send messages automatically.

**Unlimited Numbers**: New numbers being warmed up do not need to be linked to Wajom. Therefore, you can warm up an unlimited number of devices.

**Account Protection**: Warm up new WhatsApp numbers to prevent bans. Warm up old numbers that have been inactive for an extended period.

**Automated Process**: The warm-up automation sends messages to receivers automatically based on the scheduled interval.

**Receiver Flexibility**: Receivers can either reply or ignore the warm-up messages. The system will continue sending messages according to schedule.

**Warm-Up Duration**: The warm-up process runs for 30 days. After 30 days, the numbers are safe to use for marketing.

## Troubleshooting

**Number is not receiving messages**:

-   Ensure the receiver number is active and properly formatted (e.g., 60xxxxxxxxx)
-   Check to confirm the number has not been blocked

**Warm-up process stops**:

-   Check account status and ensure there are no issues with the sender number
-   Check your credit balance or subscription status

**Messages are not sent on schedule**:

-   Verify your delivery interval settings
-   Ensure the system is operating normally without interruptions

### Additional Tips from Kak Miza

**Receiver Selection**: Use active numbers that frequently reply to messages for a more effective warm-up result.

**Optimal Schedule**: Set a realistic delivery interval (not too frequent) to avoid spam detection.

**Monitoring**: Check the warm-up progress periodically to ensure everything runs smoothly.
