The Knowledge Base (_Knowledge_) is the primary reference source read by the AI Agent every time it crafts a reply for your WhatsApp customers. All information regarding your business profile, product catalog, pricing details, refund policies, payment bank accounts, and frequently asked questions (FAQs) is stored here.

## Prerequisites

✅ Your product information text, service guides, or business FAQ materials are prepared.

✅ Your account has access to a WhatsApp instance in the Wajom portal.

---

## 1. Understanding Knowledge Token Size and Capacity

1.  Open the **AI Agent** menu in the Wajom portal, then click the **Knowledge** tab.
2.  At the top of the screen, look at the **Knowledge size** indicator card:

![Knowledge Token Size Indicator on Knowledge Tab](/images/agent-ai/knowledge-01-ukuran.png)

### Knowledge Token Capacity Guidelines:

- **Unit of Measurement**: The system measures the size of knowledge materials in **tokens** (on average, 1 token is equivalent to 4 text characters). - **Full Reading (0 – 40,000 tokens)**: If your total knowledge content is below **40,000 tokens**, the entire document will be fully read by the AI for every reply message. This ensures maximum response accuracy without missing any information. - **Maximum Limit (Up to 200,000 tokens)**: The system supports storing knowledge materials up to a maximum of **200,000 tokens**.

---

## 2. Adding a Knowledge Section (+ Add Section)

To create a structured knowledge document by topic (for example: _Shipping FAQ_, _How to Order_, or _Terms & Conditions_):

1.  In the top right corner of the Knowledge page, click the blue **+ Add section** button.
2.  The add knowledge form modal will open on the screen:

![Modal Tambah Bagian Pengetahuan](/images/agent-ai/knowledge-02-tambah-bagian.png)

1.  Complete the form fields:
2.  - **Section Title**: Enter a clear and descriptive topic name (example: `Operating Hours & Shipping FAQ`).
3.  - **Knowledge Content**: Write out the information points in a structured format. Use a question-and-answer format or bullet points so it is easy for the AI model to understand.
4.  Click the **Save** button. The new knowledge section will immediately be listed under the **Knowledge sections** card.

---

## 3. Pasting Long Text (Paste Long Text)

If you already have a long SOP document, a complete catalog text, or policy articles from a website:

1.  Click the **Paste long text** button located next to the _+ Add section_ button.
2.  The text import modal will appear:

![Paste Long Text Modal for Quick Import](/images/agent-ai/knowledge-03-tempel-teks.png)

1.  Paste your copied document text into the input area.
2.  The system will automatically calculate the estimated number of tokens consumed by the text.
3.  Click the **Import** or **Save** button to add the content to your agent's knowledge base.

---

## 4. Tips for Structuring Effective Knowledge for the AI Agent

> **Tip**: - Use Question & Answer (Q&A) Format: Write common customer questions exactly as they often ask them on WhatsApp, followed by concise and informative official answers. - Specify Limitations: If a specific product is out of stock or a promotion is only valid for certain dates, state it explicitly in the knowledge content. - Update Regularly: Delete or edit outdated knowledge sections so the AI Agent does not provide expired information to potential buyers.
