## Membership Upgrade Introduction

We apologize. Your FREE Trial account will expire in 7 days. To continue enjoying Wajom's premium features, you can upgrade your FREE Trial plan to a paid Wajom plan!

For 100% full automation, Kak Miza recommends subscribing directly to the 5 WhatsApp Accounts Plan

If you need more than 5 WhatsApp accounts, please contact CS

## Explanation of the 5 WhatsApp Accounts Plan

> **Tip**: Use the same WhatsApp number to upgrade your membership. Do not change the WhatsApp number during the upgrade process.

**1st Number** - Initially, link it with your **personal number** so the personal number can act as a sender to warm up new numbers

**2nd Number** - Number for scheduled posts into our **team's WhatsApp group** (send follow-up content 5 times a day, can create chatbot autoreplies in WhatsApp pools/groups)

**3rd Number** - To send **personal messages to your own team.** Kak Miza conducts a series of daily training sessions for new agents joining Kak Miza's team

**4th Number** - Number for scheduled posts into our **prospect WhatsApp group** (send follow-up content 5 times a day, can create chatbot autoreplies in WhatsApp pools/groups). Set the AI chat to answer personal messages only. Do not reply inside the WhatsApp group. Auto closing

**5th Number** - To send **personal messages to prospects.** Kak Miza sends follow-up content to prospects once every 2-3 days via automation. Set the AI chat to answer personal messages only. Auto closing
