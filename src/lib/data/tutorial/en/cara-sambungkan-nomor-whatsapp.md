All Wajom features cannot be used until you connect at least one WhatsApp number to Wajom.

To try and test Wajom, Kak Miza recommends linking your personal number first.

When performing warm-up, make sure the sender number is your personal number—not the new number you are trying to warm up.

## Instance Information

16. FYI: This is the “Instance Information” screen.

An instance refers to the entire system within a single WhatsApp account on Wajom.

“Connected” means your Wajom account is healthy and always ready to perform tasks.

“Disconnected” means your Wajom account is inactive or disconnected, and message sending may be interrupted.

In this state, you need to click “Restart” and “Reload”.

## Kak Miza's Tips

Kak Miza's Recommendations:

1st Number - Initially link with your personal number so it can act as the sender to warm up new numbers.

2nd Number - Number to schedule posts into your **team WhatsApp groups** (send follow-up content 5 times a day, set up chatbot auto-replies within WhatsApp groups).

3rd Number - To send **personal messages to your own team.** Kak Miza conducts training series daily for new agents joining her team.

4th Number - Number to schedule posts into your **prospect WhatsApp groups** (send follow-up content 5 times a day, set up chatbot auto-replies within WhatsApp groups). Configure the AI Chat settings to reply via personal message only—never reply inside the group chat. Auto-closing.

5th Number - To send **personal messages to prospects.** Kak Miza sends automated follow-up content to prospects every 2–3 days. Set AI Chat to reply via direct message only. Auto-closing.

If you have extra budget, you can manage up to 10 WhatsApp accounts:

6th Number - First stranger number to broadcast messages to **strangers**. Rest for 2 days.

7th Number - Second stranger number to broadcast messages to **strangers**. Rest for 2 days.

8th Number - Third stranger number to broadcast messages to strangers. Rest for 2 days.

9th Number - Second prospect number (ideal for leaders receiving hundreds of leads daily). One prospect number handles 2,000 leads. Integrate AI Chat for auto-closing.

10th Number - Third prospect number (ideal for leaders receiving hundreds of leads daily). One prospect number handles 2,000 leads. Auto-closing.
