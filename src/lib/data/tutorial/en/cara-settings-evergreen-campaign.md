## Introduction to Evergreen Campaign

Campaign means a single campaign. Prospects will receive only one campaign. To send the next campaign, you need to create another campaign.

However, an “Evergreen Campaign” is a campaign series. Prospects can receive more than 1 campaign. You can send campaigns to prospects every day for 30 days or for as long as you want to send them.

-   **Full Automation**: Campaigns run automatically without manual intervention
-   **Continuous Follow-Up**: Maintain engagement with leads consistently
-   **Nurture Prospects**: Educate and guide prospects through the customer journey
-   **Maximum ROI**: A single setup for long-term results

Evergreen Campaigns are suitable for:

-   Following up with leads automatically
-   Nurturing prospects with continuous content
-   Maintaining engagement with existing customers
-   Automating the customer journey from awareness to conversion

> **Alert**: Alert! - “Evergreen Campaign” is the dream Auto Follow-Up feature for every Top Leader. It is a must to explore & master!

## Prerequisites

-   An active Wajom account with access to the campaign feature
-   A contact list that has already been prepared
-   Message content that has already been planned
-   Basic understanding of the customer journey
